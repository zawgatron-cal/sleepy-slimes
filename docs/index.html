Sleepy Slimes

A game about collecting cute slimes while you sleep.

Start a sleep session before bed, and wake up to see which slimes wandered into your zone overnight. Collect candies, fuse slimes to discover rare species, unlock new zones, and fill out your Slimepedia.

Frequently asked questions

Why didn't my sleep session count?

A session needs to be at least 4 hours long to award slimes and candies. Shorter sessions are discarded so the game can't be farmed in a few minutes.

How many slimes can I get from one night?

Up to 5 per sleep session. Longer sleep earns more candies, and the zone you pick changes which species can appear.

My alarm didn't go off.

Sleepy Slimes uses your device's local notifications for alarms. Check that notifications are allowed for Sleepy Slimes in your device settings, that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them, and that your volume isn't muted. You can review permission status in the app under Settings.

How do I get rare and legendary slimes?

Rare slimes can appear from sleeping in the right zone, but Legendary slimes only come from fusion. Check the Slimepedia for fusion hints on species you haven't discovered yet.

Will I lose my collection if I delete the app?

Yes. All progress is saved locally on your device, so deleting the app removes it permanently. There are no accounts and no cloud saves.

Can I move my save to a new phone?

Not directly. Because saves are local, a transfer only happens if your full device backup is restored onto the new phone.

Privacy

Sleepy Slimes collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no data leaves your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.

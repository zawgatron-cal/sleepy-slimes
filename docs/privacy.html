Sleepy Slimes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Sleepy Slimes is a single-player collecting game with an optional sleep timer. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not.

Summary

  • There are no accounts and no login.
  • Your game data stays on your device.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.
  • There is no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Sleepy Slimes saves your progress locally using on-device storage. This includes:

  • Sleep session records you create in the app (start time, end time, duration, and the zone you chose)
  • Your slime collection, nicknames, favorites, levels, and equipped buddy
  • Candies, streaks, unlocked zones, and Slimepedia discovery progress
  • App preferences such as sound, volume, and animation settings
  • Tutorial progress

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server we operate. If your device or platform performs its own device backup, that backup is handled by your operating system and its own privacy terms apply.

Health and sleep data

Sleep sessions in Sleepy Slimes are based only on a timer you start and stop inside the app. The app does not read from Apple Health, Google Fit, or any health, motion, microphone, or biometric sensor. Sleepy Slimes is a game and is not a medical, diagnostic, or health-tracking product, and should not be used as one.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules local alarm and reminder alerts on your device for sleep sessions. These notifications are generated on your device and are not sent through our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account identifiers
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • No usage analytics or crash telemetry

Children's privacy

Sleepy Slimes is suitable for a general audience. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Because all game data is stored on your device, deleting the app removes it. There is no server-side account for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
